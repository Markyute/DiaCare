Risk Thresholds
These are the fixed values the system uses to classify risk — the same thresholds used by the mobile app, so a patient never gets a different classification depending on which platform you check. Not user-configurable, to guarantee both stay in agreement.
Glucose Thresholds (mg/dL)
Critical High Glucose at or above this value triggers a critical alert
≥ 250 mg/dL
Elevated (At Risk) Glucose at or above this value is considered elevated
≥ 180 mg/dL
Critical Low (Hypoglycemia) Glucose below this value triggers a critical alert
< 70 mg/dL
Blood Pressure Thresholds (mmHg)
Hypertension (Critical)
BP at or above this triggers a critical alert
≥ 140 / 90 mmHg
Elevated BP (At Risk)
BP at or above this is considered elevated
≥ 120 / 80 mmHg
